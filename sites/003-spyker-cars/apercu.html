quality have defined Spyker since 1880. These same values are
reflected in the Spyker Boutique, a carefully curated collection of
lifestyle products and accessories. Each piece embodies the distinctive
character, timeless elegance and dedication to quality that lie at the
heart of the Spyker marque.

Partnerships & News

28.10.26PRESS RELEASE

Introducing Spyker Heritage: A legacy of Dutch craftsmanship and innovation since 1880.

We are proud to introduce Spyker Heritage, a new documentary series dedicated to exploring the remarkable history of Spyker. From the art of coachbuilding to pioneering automotive engineering and aviation, the series brings 146 years of Dutch craftsmanship, innovation and extraordinary stories to life.

15.10.26PRESS RELEASE

Spyker C8 Preliator XXV Coupé shortlisted for ‘Bespoke Car of the Year’ at the Prestigious International Historic Motoring Awards

The winners of the 2026 International Historic Motoring Awards will be announced on 13 November 2026, during a gala ceremony at The Peninsula London.

20.09.26EVENTS

Spyker makes its debut at The Bridge X in Bridgehampton

The Spyker C8 Preliator XXV Coupé joined an exceptional gathering of automotive heritage, craftsmanship and design at the tenth anniversary of The Bridge in the Hamptons, New York.
