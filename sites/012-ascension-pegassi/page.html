● PEGASSI

Belgian electronic artist
Pegassi, the creative alter ego of Sam Huybrecht, has emerged as
one of Europe’s most exciting next-generation acts. His sound is a
high-energy fusion of trance, techno and rave, rooted in the hip-
hop he grew up with and elevated by a signature element that
fans instantly recognize: the “bells.” What began as a sonic motif
has evolved into a defining symbol of his artistry, shaping some of
his biggest live moments.

A:

How one track started it.

1

Pegassi’s rise accelerated with his
breakout club anthem “Yoyoyo,” a track
that set the tone for the hybrid,
emotionally-charged rave identity he
would continue perfecting. Follow-up
singles like “Spectrals Bells” and the
explosive “227kg” amplified his
momentum; the latter became a true cult
phenomenon, earning millions of streams
and cementing the “bells” as an essential
part of his sonic DNA.

B:

An international following

2

His catalogue has grown into a global
streaming presence, including “Bring It
Back,” released on Berlin’s iconic Live
From Earth label. On stage, Pegassi has
established himself across Europe’s most
respected festivals and clubs, performing
at Awakenings, Time Warp,
Tomorrowland, RSO, Dour, Sziget,
Intercell, Pitch, BTV, Glitch, Hive, Baum,
AVA and Boiler Room. His global footprint
continues to expand through strong
worldwide touring, with yearly runs
across Europe, Australia, North America
and South America that have earned him
a devoted international following.
