Creative communications agency in Geneva

Independent creative agency based in Geneva since 2011. We partner with local and international brands and institutions to build their brand, digital presence and campaigns.

What we do

When every brand follows the same codes, they all end up looking alike. The strongest ones are built from what is fundamentally true to them, not from what is expected of them. We work with clients who share that ambition. Together, we build brands capable of embedding themselves in culture and lasting beyond trends. Brands you feel, not just see.
