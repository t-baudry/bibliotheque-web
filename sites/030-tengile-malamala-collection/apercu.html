A Thriving Wilderness

Along one of the most wildlife-rich stretches of the Sand River in the Sabi Sand region, Tengile MalaMala Collection brings together three lodges and three camps. Located in Mpumalanga, South Africa, this wilderness has been protected for nearly a century and now enters a new chapter of focused stewardship and long-term investment.

We stand for balance where people and wildlife thrive together. Rooted in partnership with the warm-hearted Shangaan community, our ethos ensures that when our people prosper, so too does our wilderness.

Accommodation

Our lodges and camps each offer a distinct expression of the wilderness, from earthy traditional and nostalgic charm to contemporary sophistication. Set along the Sand River in the Sabi Sand area, this land is renowned for what it effortlessly attracts: an extraordinary abundance of wildlife. From the lodges and camps, guests enjoy exceptional vantage points from which to experience the pulse of the Greater Kruger.

Lodges

Tengile River Lodge, Rattray’s Lodge and Khensani River Lodge offer privacy, generous proportions and distinctive interiors. Each suite opens onto the Sand River, with private plunge pools, indoor and outdoor showers and spacious living areas. Intentionally intimate game drives allow guests to experience remarkable wildlife at a personal pace, without compromising on the classic rhythm of safari.

Our story

With roots reaching back to 1927, our story is one of stewardship, partnership and respect for nature: a philosophy guided by the Shangaan principle of Ntumbuluko, meaning deep harmony with the land.

Today, under shared community ownership, the Tengile MalaMala Collection continues to protect its wilderness and empower its people.
