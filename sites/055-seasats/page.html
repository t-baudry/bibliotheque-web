Lightfish

Our flagship autonomous vessel. Light. Simple.

Modular. Extremely effective. A satellite at sea for

gathering vast ocean intelligence without the

cost or complexity of a crewed expedition.

The vessel, in brief

Lightfish offers range and resilience—six months, 8,000 nautical miles—on solar-electric power with hybrid reserve. Rugged and self-righting, it operates in seas up to state 6, with collision avoidance, GPS-denied navigation, and redundant comms for global reach. Most payloads bolt on and plug in in minutes. Portable enough to launch by hand and simple enough to be used by anyone.

  • Persistent

    Up to 6 months. Solar electric with hybrid reserve.

  • Portable

    Fits in a pickup truck. Deployable by hand.

  • Modular

    Payloads swapped in minutes. C2 interoperable.

  • Rugged

    Operational in Sea State 6 conditions.

  • Affordable

    Designed affordable for deployment at scale.

  • Simple

    Intuitive Operations. 3-5 Days of Training.

Quickfish

Tactical, agile and swift. Launches in seconds.

Operates for days. A rapid response vessel for

intercepting or interdicting maritime threats.

The vessel, in brief

The Quickfish permits speed on water and land alike. Its COTS design makes it fast to build and quick to maintain. Its simplicity means it takes just 3 days training. And its powerful propulsion means it can exceed 40 knots. Launched remotely from dock or mooring, it can monitor, intercept and ID anywhere from ports to oceans. Its internal drone platform can extend its presence and capabilities for closer inspections or as a show of force.

  • Swift

    Sprints 35+ knots. Cruise 400+ nm.

  • Modular

  • Rugged

    Operational in Sea State 6 conditions.

  • Reliable

    Resilient to GPS jamming and spoofing, with anti-tamper capabilities available.

Heavyfish

The mighty. Built for power, endurance, and

adaptability. Can tow payloads such as heavy

acoustic arrays up to 1000 pounds, travel for

months on end, and cruise up to 8.5 knots.


Expected Splash Date: 2027

The vessel, in brief

The Heavyfish is built on the proven foundation of its sibling vessels, providing additional brawn, speed and payload possibilities, for wider use cases and added flexibility. Pairing electric and diesel drive into a single propulsion unit for lasting performance and endurance. Just as rugged, modular and simple as its siblings. Ideal for MDA, ASW, sonar surveys, harbor security and HVU escort.
