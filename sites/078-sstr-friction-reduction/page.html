Process

Friction reduction while drilling. Proven by data.

We follow four key steps to be confident of success in challenging runs

SSTR SYSTEMS

Friction and vibration act in
different zones — the
solution has to work in
every one of them

Combined, these solutions significantly extend
lateral reach while drilling

FRS

FRS isolate the drill-pipe
tool joint from the
borehole wall, reduce
torque and drag, increase
weight transfer in slide
drilling, and eliminate
surface torsional and
lateral vibrations.

Spacing is set by the high side-load intervals

TRANSFER X1

Transfer X1 protects the
drill-pipe tool joint from
abrasion. Wear-resistant,
designed for the open-hole
interval

TRANSFER X6

Transfer X6 damps
torsional and lateral
vibrations in the BHA.
Protects the mud motor /
RSS, MWD and LWD tools
from failure. Converts
radial load into additional
axial load.

TRANSFER X3

DRILL BIT

The PDC bit cuts the rock at
the bottomhole. SSTR
equipment works along the
entire drill string —
reducing friction and
vibration to preserve bit
life and wellpath accuracy.

Products

SSTR equipment

// FRS

Frameless composite friction reducers. Installed on the drill pipe in zones of maximum side load. The pipe rotates inside a stationary composite sleeve pressed against the wellbore wall.

  • 01

    Torque reduction at the rotary table / top drive

  • 02

    More weight on bit transferred downhole

  • 03

    Lower hook load when pulling out of hole

  • 04

    Less drilling vibration in the installation zone

  • 05

    Drill pipe and casing wear protection

Learn moreLearn more

// SVR

Shock and vibration reducer for the BHA. Wear-resistant composite sleeves stay stationary against the wall; the sub rotates inside.

  • 01

    Lateral and torsional vibration damping in the BHA

  • 02

    MWD/LWD and RSS protection from vibration

  • 03

    Torque reduction in the installation zone

  • 04

    Longer bit and BHA component life

Learn moreLearn more

// TRANSFER X3

TRANSFER multifunctional sub with stabilizing floating sections. Run in the open hole to damp vibrations and protect the drill pipe and BHA components.

  • 01

    BHA only — no surface Transfer sections

  • 02

    Drilling with high WOB

  • 03

    Suppress stick-slip and axial shocks at the bottom of the string

  • 04

    Protect PDC bits from cutter chipping
