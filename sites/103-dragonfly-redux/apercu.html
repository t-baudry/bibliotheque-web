Stablecoins and the Collapse of the Legacy Payment Model
Stablecoins aren’t just an improvement on or connective tissue for existing payment rails, they allow companies to bypass those legacy rails entirely and collapse the current payments value chain in on itself. Because if you’re stablecoin native, everything is just a book transfer. A new class of companies are being born (or are adapting) today that understand this — and they will drive the re-architecting of how money moves.
